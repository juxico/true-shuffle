True Shuffle is currently provided for internal use only. It is an independent tool that uses Spotify's public developer platform. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Spotify.

1. Who we are

[Company Name], LLC ("we," "us" or "our"), a [State] limited liability company, provides True Shuffle (the "App"), a web page that creates truly random orderings of your Spotify music and lets you control Spotify playback. This policy explains what information the App uses, why and the choices you have.

2. The short version

  • We do not run servers that collect or store your information. The App runs in your web browser.
  • The App talks directly from your browser to Spotify, and to a public lyrics service when you view lyrics.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information, and we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • Signing out removes everything the App saved about you from that browser.

3. Information the App uses

When you sign in, Spotify asks you to approve the App's access. With your approval, the App can use the following, only to provide its features:

Spotify informationWhy the App uses it
Your Spotify user IDTo find and manage the App's playlists in your account.
Your Liked Songs and playlists you own or collaborate onTo build shuffled mixes from the songs you choose.
Playlist creation and editingTo create and update two private playlists in your account: "True Shuffle" (the current mix) and "True Shuffle · history" (recently used songs, so new mixes avoid repeats).
Current playback, queue, devices and recently played songsTo show what is playing, the queue and recent songs, and to play, pause, skip, seek and switch devices when you ask.

The App does not request your email address, password, payment details, location, contacts or followers. You sign in on Spotify's own page, and the App never sees your Spotify password.

4. Where information is stored

  • In your browser: the App saves sign-in tokens issued by Spotify, your settings (such as selected playlists and mix length) and short-term copies of song lists to make the App faster. This stays on your device in your browser's local storage. We cannot see it.
  • In your Spotify account: the two private playlists described above. You can delete them in Spotify at any time.
  • With us: nothing. We do not keep a database of users, listening history or account details.

5. Third-party services

The App relies on these services. Each handles information under its own privacy policy:

  • Spotify provides sign-in, your music data and playback control. See Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free community lyrics library. When you view lyrics, the App sends the current song's title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB to look them up. No account information is sent.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the App's files. Like any website host, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address when you load the page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

When you sign in or out, the App briefly opens Spotify's sign-out page in a small window. This clears any Spotify account previously signed in on that browser, so another person's account details are not shown to you.

6. Cookies and tracking

The App does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies. Spotify, LRCLIB and GitHub may use cookies or logs on their own services as described in their policies.

7. How long information is kept

Information saved in your browser stays until you sign out, clear your browser data, or the Spotify sign-in expires. Playlists created in your Spotify account stay until you delete them.

8. Your choices

  • Sign out in the App to remove everything it saved in that browser.
  • Remove the App's access to your Spotify account at any time at spotify.com/account/apps.
  • Delete the App's playlists in Spotify if you no longer want them.

Because we do not store your information, we generally have nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a request or question, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required to have a Spotify account in your country, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Security

The App uses Spotify's secure sign-in method (OAuth with PKCE) over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and it never handles your Spotify password. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please sign out on shared devices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, give notice in the App.
